Welcome to MusicLock
Lock your phone while music plays and a beautiful player takes over your lock screen — controls, art, the works.

We need a few permissions first. We'll explain each one.
🔔
Notifications
Allow MusicLock to post notifications.
Why

The lock-screen player is launched as a full-screen notification — without this it can't appear when your screen turns on.

Required
📡
Notification access
Let MusicLock read media notifications.
Why

This is how we know what's playing — title, artist, art — and how the play/pause/skip controls work. Opens a system settings page.

Required
🪟
Display over other apps
Allow drawing on top of the lock screen.
Why

The player draws over the system lock screen so your controls sit right where you expect them. Opens a system settings page.

Required
⛶
Full-screen notifications
Allow the immersive full-screen player.
Why

Lets the player take the whole screen the moment your phone wakes, instead of a small notification banner. Opens a system settings page.

Required
🔋
Ignore battery optimization
Keep MusicLock running in the background.
Why

Stops the system from killing the listener while idle, so playback is never missed. Optional — the app still works, just less reliably.

Optional
Pick a look
Choose a lock-screen player and color theme. You can change these anytime.
